Example information security baseline

This is an illustrative text written for the CLHEAR walkthrough. It is not a law or a published standard.

Part 1
Governance

Section 1. Every organisation shall appoint a person responsible for information security and record the appointment.

Section 2. The management body shall approve the information security policy and review it at least once a year.

Part 2
Controls

Section 3. Every organisation shall keep an inventory of the systems that store or process customer data.

Section 4. Every organisation shall:
(a) grant access to systems only to people who need it for their role;
(b) review user access rights at least every six months;
(c) remove access within two working days of a person leaving.

Section 5. Where an organisation processes personal data, it shall encrypt that data when it is stored and when it is sent over public networks.

Section 6. Every organisation shall keep a log of security incidents and shall notify affected customers without undue delay where an incident is likely to harm them.

Section 7. Staff should complete security awareness training when they join and every year after that.

Part 3
Supervision

Section 8. The supervisory authority shall publish guidance on the application of this baseline.
