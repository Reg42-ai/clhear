What We Are Building

Reg42, and where we are starting

Reg42 exists to give compliance a single source of truth, first in financial services and later in other industries. Our tagline puts it simply: your entire compliance program, one intelligent system. The work happens in three steps.

  1. Map. CLHEAR sets out what the rules require for a given kind of company. We call that the blueprint.
  2. Compare. Our system connects to what the company actually runs, its policies, roles, committees, systems, and evidence, and describes it in the same language. The gap between what is required and what exists is the diagnosis.
  3. Comply. Software and AI agents close the gaps, each one judged by the obligation it serves. Accountable people sign off.

The reference blueprint is free. Reg42 is the diagnosis and the cure.

We are starting with AI rules across the US, the EU, and the UK together, because that tests the core idea directly. Adding a country should not multiply the program.

The EU moved these deadlines almost two years after the Act entered into force. In a program mapped clause by clause, a change like this shows up as a change to specific obligations, and to everything linked to them.

Our starting points today include FINRA Rule 2210, FINRA Rule 5310, and the EU AI Act. Our first focus is global brokers and trading platforms.

This is the UK version of the duty in FINRA Rule 2210, as the FCA supervises it. In 2022, the FCA’s work with lenders and brokers alone led to 7,191 promotions being changed or withdrawn. In 2024, its work with claims management firms led to 9,197.

We hold ourselves to three targets, and they are targets, not results yet: cutting a firm’s total compliance cost by 80%, keeping a reliable compliance score at any moment, and answering any compliance question within sixty seconds.
